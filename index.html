Knowledge agents for science

Your team knows more than it has written down.

Pica builds knowledge agents for research teams. They turn lab notes, messages, GitHub repos, databases, and papers into a shared, cited knowledge base, capturing what teams know but rarely write down or share.

Most of what a research team knows is siloed and never written up.
  • It's scatteredResults live in lab notes, decisions in chat threads, methods in code, data in databases, and context in papers. No one place holds the whole picture.
  • It's tacitWhy an approach was dropped, which dataset version to trust, what was already tried: it's rarely captured, and it leaves when people do.
  • It's invisible to AI toolsInternal knowledge isn't in any model's training data, and search can find a document but not tell you what your team actually concluded.
What Pica does

A shared knowledge base that maintains itself.

Pica's agents work in the background, reading what your team produces and keeping a living record of what it knows.

Captures what's known

Agents pull findings, decisions, and methods out of everyday work and organize them into linked notes, so knowledge builds up instead of getting lost.

Cited and checked

Every claim links back to where it came from. Pica weighs how well each finding is supported and flags where sources disagree.

Kept current

As work moves on, knowledge goes stale or gets superseded. Pica notices when the underlying evidence changes and updates what depends on it.

An example

"Have we tried this before?"

A new team member wants to try an idea. Instead of asking around, they ask Pica.

Where you use it

Knowledge you own, in the tools you already use.

Your markdown vault

The knowledge base lives as plain markdown files, so it works with Obsidian and git. You can read, edit, and keep all of it.

Web app

Ask questions, browse the knowledge map, and see the sources behind each answer, including what conflicts and what's out of date.

API for your agents

Your own agents can query the knowledge base and contribute to it, with sources attached to every answer.

Who it's for

Built for research teams.

Research labs

A lab memory that doesn't graduate

Results, failed attempts, and know-how are spread across people, notebooks, and threads, and they leave when people move on.

Pica keeps the lab's working knowledge in one place, current and cited.

Clinical research

Evidence you can trace

Clinical knowledge is spread across protocols, internal datasets, and fast-moving literature, and much of it can't leave the organization.

Pica ties every finding to its source and flags what has been superseded.

How it works

From scattered sources to a shared knowledge base.

Connect

Link lab notes, messaging, GitHub, databases, and paper libraries.

Capture

Agents extract findings, decisions, and methods, and link them to their sources.

Maintain

As new work lands, agents reconcile conflicts and retire what's out of date.

Answer

Ask in plain language and get a cited answer, or a clear note that the team doesn't know yet.
