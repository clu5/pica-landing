Your knowledge is everywhere.

Notes in one app. Papers in another. Code on GitHub. Decisions buried in Slack. The reasons you dropped an idea live only in your head, until you forget them.

Pica keeps track, so you don't have to.

Connects your sources

Does the filing

Agents turn what you read and do into linked notes.

Shows its sources

Checks itself

Your knowledge stays yours.

Pica writes plain markdown to a folder you choose. Open it in Obsidian, keep it in git, or take it with you. Share with your lab only when you want to.

Why we're building this.

Soon, most knowledge work will be done by agents in the background, reading and writing for each other. That only works if what they write down can be trusted.

We're starting with the knowledge that matters most to you: your own.
