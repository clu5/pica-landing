The problem

Research knowledge is siloed.

Scattered

Across notes, chats, code, data, and papers.

Unwritten

Why things were tried, dropped, or trusted.

Lost

It leaves when people do.

Vision
Knowledge agents will work in the background, at scale, keeping a shared knowledge layer for every research team.
  • Agents mostly talk to agentsPeople meet the knowledge layer through a few simple interfaces. Most reading and writing happens between agents.
  • So trust is the foundationOne agent's mistake becomes another agent's input. The knowledge layer has to be more reliable than any agent writing to it.
  • Knowledge should compoundEach question should leave the knowledge base better, so the next answer is faster, cheaper, and more grounded.
What Pica does

A knowledge base that maintains itself.

Captures

Findings, decisions, and methods from everyday work.

Cites

Every claim links to its source.

Checks

Weighs evidence and flags conflicts.

Updates

Retires what's stale or superseded.
