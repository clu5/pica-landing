What it does

A research collaborator that has read everything relevant.

The literature grows faster than anyone can read it. Pica's agents do the reading, keep track of the evidence, and show their work.

Deep literature search

Ask a research question in plain language. Agents search across papers, preprints, and data, and follow citations until the trail runs out.

Living knowledge maps

Gaps and next steps

Research

Agents produce findings fast. Knowing which to trust is harder.

Pica grew out of our research on uncertainty, search, and experimental design. These are some of the failure modes we build against.

Absence of evidence

Right number, wrong question

When a search comes up empty, how likely is it that the thing exists? Nearly half of the 58 language models we tested gave a different number: the chance that the search missed it. The number looks calibrated but answers a different question.

Pica reasons about how much a search could have found before it treats "nothing found" as evidence.

Independence

Agreement isn't confirmation

Five agents can report the same improvement from one shared run, and models tend to over-count repeated evidence. That's one observation reported five times, not five pieces of support.

Pica tracks where each piece of evidence came from and counts it once.

Selection

The best result hides the search

A result that was the best of forty configurations can reproduce perfectly and still fail on new data. Rerunning it doesn't reveal how it was chosen.

Pica keeps the record of attempts behind a finding, not just the winner.

Experimental design

Spend checks where they matter

A new run, fresh evaluation data, or an audit of the search each cost compute or expert time, and each answers a different doubt.

Pica picks the check expected to improve the conclusion most per unit of cost.
