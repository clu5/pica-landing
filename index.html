Knowledge agents for science

Turn scattered research into working knowledge.

For research labs and clinical research groups. Pica's agents work in the background, refining your experiments, notes, and papers into a shared knowledge layer, checking each other's work, and keeping it current as the evidence changes.

The knowledge that matters most in science is distributed, specialized, and internal.
  • It isn't in the training dataExperiment logs, run configs, lab notebooks, protocols, unpublished results, and clinical datasets are invisible to general-purpose models.
  • It isn't always factualIt's full of hypotheses, judgment calls, and results that later get superseded.
  • Today's tools start from scratchRetrieval finds what exists but can't separate evidence from noise. Agents re-read every source on every question and leave nothing behind.
What Pica does

A knowledge layer that gets better with use.

Most knowledge agents will mostly talk to each other, and meet people only through simple interfaces. That makes trust in the shared layer the thing to get right.

A knowledge refinery

Agents turn raw sources into durable structure: extracted findings, indexes, crosswalks between datasets, and documentation of each source. Each question becomes cheaper and better answered than the last. Knowledge compounds.

Reliable from unreliable parts

Every finding is scored by how much independent evidence supports it. Five agents repeating one shared run count once, and the search behind a "best" result stays on the record.

Kept current

As new work lands, parts of any knowledge base go stale or get superseded. Pica tracks what depends on what and rechecks when the evidence underneath shifts.

An example

One finding, three agents, forty runs.

Your agents run ML experiments overnight. In the morning, three of them report that a new augmentation improves accuracy. Should you believe it?

Reports3 agents report the improvement
Provenance2 of the 3 reports come from the same training run, so there are 2 independent reports, not 3
SelectionThe reported config was the best of 40 tried, and the other 39 were never written up
AssessmentSupport is weaker than it looks. The main doubt is selection, not noise
Next checkEvaluate on fresh held-out data before spending compute on more reruns, since rerunning the winner can't reveal how it was chosen

Where you use it

Knowledge you own, in the tools you already use.

Your markdown vault

The knowledge layer lives as plain markdown files, so it works with Obsidian and git. Agents write and maintain linked notes, each claim tied to its source. You can read, edit, and keep all of it.

Web app

Ask questions, browse the knowledge map, and see the evidence behind each finding: what supports it, what conflicts with it, and what has gone stale.

API for your agents

Your own agents query the knowledge layer and contribute to it. Answers come back with sources and how well supported they are, so one agent's error doesn't quietly become another's input.

Who it's for

Built for labs and clinical research.

For research labs

A lab's memory that doesn't graduate

Results, failed attempts, and know-how are spread across students' notebooks, Slack threads, and experiment logs, and they leave when people do.

Pica keeps a lab's working knowledge in one place, current and cited, for people and their agents.

For clinical research

Evidence you can trace

Clinical knowledge is spread across protocols, internal datasets, and fast-moving literature, and it can't be pooled into a public model.

Pica ties every finding to its source and flags what has been superseded. It's built by a team that has shipped an FDA-cleared clinical AI device.

How it works

From scattered sources to grounded answers.

Connect

Point Pica at papers, experiment logs, notebooks, datasets, and internal documents.

Refine

Background agents decide what's worth building, within a budget, and maintain it against drift.

Check

Findings are weighed by independent support, and the next check goes where it would change the most.

Answer

Every claim is cited to its source. When the knowledge isn't there, Pica says so and names the gap.

Research

Reliable knowledge from unreliable agents.

Pica grows out of research at the MIT Media Lab on how agent-written knowledge can be more reliable than any agent that contributes to it.

Economics

Knowledge structure is capital

Agents work under real budgets of tokens, tool calls, and time, and most of the value sits in a small share of sources.

Pica treats what agents build as an investment: chosen for expected value, maintained, and retired when it stops paying off.

Independence

Agreement isn't confirmation

Agents can agree without adding any independent support, and models tend to over-count repeated evidence.

Pica records where each piece of evidence came from and counts it once.

Selection

The best result hides the search

A result that was the best of forty attempts can reproduce perfectly and still fail on new data.

Pica keeps the history behind a finding, not just the winner.

Experimental design

Spend checks where they matter

A rerun, fresh data, and an audit each cost something and each answer a different doubt.

Pica picks the check expected to improve the conclusion most per unit of cost.
