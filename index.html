What it does

A research collaborator that has read everything relevant.

The literature grows faster than anyone can read it. Pica's agents do the reading, keep track of the evidence, and show their work.

Deep literature search

Ask a research question in plain language. Agents search across papers, preprints, and data, and follow citations until the trail runs out.

Living knowledge maps

Claims, methods, datasets, and results are linked into a structured map of a field that updates as new work is published.

Gaps and next steps

Pica reports what's well supported, where findings conflict, and which questions nobody has tested yet — with a clear sense of how hard it looked.

How it works

From question to grounded answer.

Ask

Start with a question, a hypothesis, or a paper you want to build on.

Read

Agents gather and read the relevant literature, extracting claims and the evidence behind them.

Connect

Findings are reconciled across sources: agreements, contradictions, and missing links.

Answer

You get a cited synthesis, a map of the evidence, and concrete open questions.

Named for the magpie, Pica pica — a bird that gathers what it finds and remembers where it put it.
  • Every claim is citedAnswers link back to the exact passages they came from.
  • Calibrated about absence"We found nothing" means something only when the search could have found it. Pica tells you how thorough it was.
  • Built for researchersDesigned with working scientists, for the way research actually happens.
