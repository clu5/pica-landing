About

Named for the magpie, Pica pica.

The Eurasian magpie is one of the most intelligent birds, and among the most intelligent of all animals. It is one of the few species known to recognize itself in a mirror. It uses tools, and it stores food across seasons and remembers where it hid it.

Pica builds agents with the same instincts for science: they gather what a field knows, remember where each piece came from, and keep it reliable as it changes.

Science doesn't need more generated text. It needs knowledge it can rely on.
  • Every claim is citedAnswers link back to the exact sources they came from.
  • Honest about gapsWhen the knowledge isn't there, Pica says so and says where it looked.
  • Built for researchersMade for the way research actually happens, with knowledge that is distributed, specialized, and still being worked out.

Research on data acquisition, distributed uncertainty quantification, and how language models report what they find, published at NeurIPS and ICML. NSF Graduate Research Fellow.
