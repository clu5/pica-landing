About

Named for the magpie, Pica pica.

The Eurasian magpie is one of the most intelligent birds, and among the most intelligent of all animals. It is one of the few species known to recognize itself in a mirror. It uses tools, and it stores food across seasons and remembers where it hid it.

Pica builds agents with the same instincts for research teams: they gather what a team knows, remember where each piece came from, and keep it reliable as it changes.

Research teams don't need more generated text. They need knowledge they can rely on.
  • Every claim is citedAnswers link back to the exact sources they came from.
  • Honest about gapsWhen the knowledge isn't there, Pica says so.
  • Yours to keepYour knowledge base is plain markdown you own, not locked inside a tool.
