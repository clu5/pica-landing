Approach

Knowledge you can rely on.

A knowledge base written by agents is only useful if people can trust it. Pica is built around a few principles for keeping agent-maintained knowledge accurate, traceable, and up to date.

01

Capture what isn't written down

The most valuable team knowledge is often tacit: why a decision was made, what was tried and abandoned, which version of a dataset to use.

Pica's agents look for this knowledge in everyday work and turn it into durable, linked notes.

02

Every claim has a source

A knowledge base is only as good as your ability to check it.

Each entry links back to the notes, messages, code, data, or papers it came from, so anyone can verify it in a click.

03

Weigh the evidence

The same result repeated in many places isn't the same as many independent results, and agreement isn't always confirmation.

Pica tracks where evidence comes from, surfaces disagreements, and points to the checks that would settle them.

04

Keep it current

Research moves fast. Findings get revised, methods change, and old conclusions quietly go stale.

Pica tracks what depends on what and flags knowledge that's outdated or superseded.

05

Be honest about gaps

"Nothing found" is only meaningful if the search could have found it.

When the knowledge isn't there, Pica says so and shows where it looked.

06

Spend effort where it counts

Agents work within real limits on time and compute.

Pica focuses on the sources and questions that matter most to your team, and builds structure that makes every later question faster to answer.
